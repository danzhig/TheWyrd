Every row with one end in Jeremiah's path sections and the other in another book, grouped by the two sections it joins and ranked by total votes (at least 10; the top 300 shown). These signals suggest a type; the write-ups are drafted only for the pairs that hold up, and each needs the owner's approval before it is shown.
