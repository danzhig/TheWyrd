## `rut02` The generations from Perez to David

- **Ruth.4.18-Ruth.4.22 → Luke.3.31-Luke.3.33** · allusion · strength 3 · verified (approved 2026-11-16) · fingerprint `4a4daa0918b0d465`
- From: The Redeemer (`ruth-04`)
- To: The Genealogy of Jesus (`luke-x1`)

> Luke 3:31–33 recalls Ruth 4:18–22: the line from Perez to David, set into Jesus' genealogy. Ruth closes with the generations from Perez to David: Hezron, Ram, Amminadab, Nahshon, Salmon, Boaz, Obed and Jesse. Luke's genealogy of Jesus, read in the other direction, passes through the same names, with the Septuagint's spelling of Nahshon (below).

## `rut03` Under his wings

- **Ruth.2.12 → Luke.13.34** · echo · strength 1 · verified (approved 2026-11-16) · fingerprint `85a205b3cfd526f1`
- From: The Field of Boaz (`ruth-02`)

> Luke 13:34 echoes Ruth 2:12: Jesus longs to gather Jerusalem as a hen gathers her brood under her wings, the wings Ruth took refuge under. Boaz blesses Ruth in the name of the God of Israel, under whose wings she has come to take refuge.
