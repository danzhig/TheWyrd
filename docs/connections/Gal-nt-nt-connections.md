## `gal-l01` Abraham believed God

- **Gal.3.6-Gal.3.9 → Rom.4.1-Rom.4.3** · parallel · strength 3 · draft · fingerprint `9b3d6735a22e83f8`
- From: Faith, the Law and Sonship (`gal-02`, library)
- To: Abraham’s Faith; Peace with God; Adam and Christ (`rom-02`, library)

> Galatians 3:6–9 and Romans 4:1–3 both tell that Abraham believed God and it was counted to him as righteousness, quoting Genesis to show that righteousness comes through faith.
