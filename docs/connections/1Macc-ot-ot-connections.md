## `1macc-o01` Abraham found faithful when tested

- **Gen.22.1-Gen.22.14 → 1Macc.2.52** · allusion · strength 3 · verified (approved 2026-11-20) · fingerprint `4742c4fcb5368a1b`
- From: Isaac Born, Abraham Tested (`gen-10`)
- To: Mattathias Revolts (`1macc-02`)
- Order: older end from; the later end uses it; basis text

> 1 Maccabees 2:52 recalls Genesis 22:1–14: in his last words Mattathias asks whether Abraham was not found faithful when tested, and it was counted to him as righteousness. Genesis tells of God testing Abraham by asking him to offer Isaac. Mattathias joins that test to the words Genesis uses of Abraham's faith, as the first of a line of ancestors he holds up to his sons.

- 1Macc.2.52 carries: Abraham

- From: The Fiery Furnace (`dan-03`)
- To: Mattathias Revolts (`1macc-02`)

> 1 Maccabees 2:59 recalls Daniel 3:19–27: the three who believed, Hananiah, Azariah and Mishael, were saved from the fire.
