> 1 Samuel 2:1–10 and Psalms 113:7–9 both tell of the LORD who lifts the poor from the dust and the needy from the ash heap to sit with princes. > 1 Chronicles 10:1–14 retells 1 Samuel 31:1–13: the Philistines defeat Israel on Mount Gilboa, Saul's sons fall, and Saul dies by his own sword.
