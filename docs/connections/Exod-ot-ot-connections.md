## `exod-o07` The Ten Commandments, given twice

- From: Sinai and the Ten Commandments (`exod-09`)
- To: The Ten Commandments Repeated (`deut-x104`)
- Order: older end from; the later end uses it; basis text

> Deuteronomy 5:6–21 retells Exodus 20:1–17: Moses repeats the Ten Commandments to the people about to enter the land. The commandments run in the same order with nearly the same words, and the Hebrew shares rare words such as those for the image and for coveting (below).
