## `deut-o01` With all his heart, soul and might

- **Deut.6.4-Deut.6.5 → 2Kgs.23.25** · allusion · strength 3 · draft · fingerprint `792bd393bc7be3c8`
- From: The Shema (`deut-01`)
- To: Manasseh and Josiah's Reform (`2kgs-09`)
- Order: older end from; the later end uses it; basis text

> 2 Kings 23:25 recalls Deuteronomy 6:4–5: Josiah turned to the LORD with all his heart, soul and might, according to all the law of Moses. Deuteronomy commands Israel to love the LORD with heart, soul and might; Kings gives Josiah the same three, and says no king before or after was like him.
