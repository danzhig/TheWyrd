Written by `pnpm connections:propose Num` from OpenBible.info's cross-references (CC BY 4.0), pinned in `sources/manifest.json`. Every row with one end in Numbers's path sections and the other in another book, grouped by the two sections it joins and ranked by total votes (at least 10; the top 300 shown). For each, the strongest row's passages are compared: the longest run of words they share in WEBC (a quotation shows as a long run), the names both carry, and, between the Testaments, uncommon Greek lemmas the Septuagint and TAGNT share at their rows. (The Septuagint transcription has no lemma for some words, such as the capitals that open a book, so a missing Greek signal is not proof of absence.) These signals suggest a type; the write-ups are drafted only for the pairs that hold up, and each needs the owner's approval before it is shown.
