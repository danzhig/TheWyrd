## `neh-o01` Booths, found written in the law

- **Lev.23.39-Lev.23.43 → Neh.8.14-Neh.8.17** · quotation · strength 3 · verified (approved 2026-11-20) · fingerprint `95f7c0b8db5902d9`
- From: The Feasts of the Year (`lev-14`, library)
- To: Ezra Reads the Law (`neh-08`)
- Order: older end from; the later end uses it; basis text

> Nehemiah 8:14–17 quotes Leviticus 23:39–43: the people find written in the law that Israel should live in booths during the feast of the seventh month. Leviticus commands the feast with branches of trees, as a memorial that Israel lived in booths after the exodus. Nehemiah tells how the people gathered branches, built booths on roofs and in courtyards, and kept the feast as they had not since Joshua's day (the shared words below).

## `neh-o02` The sea divided, confessed

> Nehemiah 9:9–11 recalls Exodus 14:21–28: God divided the sea before Israel so that they passed through on dry ground, and threw their pursuers into the depths. Exodus tells of the east wind, the dry ground and the waters returning on Pharaoh's army. The prayer of Nehemiah 9 tells it with the same words for the dry ground and the dividing (below), the pursuers thrown like a stone into mighty waters.
