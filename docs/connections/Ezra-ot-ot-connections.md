> Ezra 1:1–3 and 2 Chronicles 36:22–23 both tell of Cyrus's decree in the first year of his reign: the LORD stirred his spirit to build him a house in Jerusalem, and his people may go up. ## `ezra-o02` Cyrus, who will say of Jerusalem: let it be built

- **Isa.44.28 → Ezra.1.1-Ezra.1.2** · echo · strength 2 · verified (approved 2026-11-20) · fingerprint `c67a2b953ea8bf53`
- From: Israel the Witness; Idols Mocked; Cyrus (`isa-13`, library)
- To: Cyrus's Decree and the First Return (`ezra-01`)
- Order: older end unknown; no use claimed; basis unstated

> Isaiah 44:28 and Ezra 1:1–2 both tell of Cyrus as the one through whom Jerusalem and its temple will be built. Isaiah has the LORD call Cyrus his shepherd, who will say of Jerusalem that it shall be built and of the temple that its foundation shall be laid. Ezra records Cyrus's decree that the LORD has charged him to build him a house in Jerusalem.
