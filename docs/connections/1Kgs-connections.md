## `kgs05` God feeds the ravens

- **1Kgs.17.4-1Kgs.17.6 → Luke.12.24** · echo · strength 1 · verified (approved 2026-11-19) · fingerprint `8065b0c23da3ae95`
- From: Elijah: Drought, the Widow, and Carmel (`1kgs-08`)
- To: Toward Jerusalem: the Samaritan, Martha and Mary (`luke-10`)

> Luke 12:24 echoes 1 Kings 17:4–6: consider the ravens, which God feeds, as he fed Elijah by ravens at the brook. During the drought God sends Elijah to a brook where the ravens bring him bread and meat morning and evening. Jesus tells his disciples to consider the ravens, which neither sow nor reap, yet God feeds them; his word for the ravens, found nowhere else in the New Testament, is the Septuagint's (below).
