> 2 Maccabees 6:18–20 recalls Leviticus 11:7–8: the aged scribe Eleazar, forced to open his mouth for swine's flesh, spits it out and goes to torture rather than taste what is not lawful. ## `2macc-l02` Fire from heaven for Moses

- **Lev.9.23-Lev.9.24 → 2Macc.2.10-2Macc.2.11** · allusion · strength 3 · draft · fingerprint `4fb2be58cbd00ff0`
- From: The Priests Ordained (`lev-04`, library)
- To: Letters to the Jews in Egypt; the Author’s Preface (`2macc-01`, library)
- Order: older end from; the later end uses it; basis text

> 2 Maccabees 2:10–11 names Leviticus 9:23–24: the letter at the start of Second Maccabees tells how fire came down and consumed the sacrifice when Moses prayed. Leviticus tells it at the priests' ordination, the fire from before the LORD consuming the offering and the people falling on their faces. The letter adds Moses' word about the sin offering that was not eaten.

- 2Macc.2.10 carries: Moses, fire
