## `esth-o01` The day before Mordecai's day

- **Esth.9.20-Esth.9.22 → 2Macc.15.36** · allusion · strength 3 · draft · fingerprint `9909fa5c1ce74df1`
- From: Purim; the Dream Explained (`esth-10`)
- To: Nicanor and the Final Victory (`2macc-13`, library)
- Order: older end from; the later end uses it; basis text

> 2 Maccabees 15:36 names the feast of Esther 9:20–22: the day of victory over Nicanor falls on the thirteenth of Adar, the day before Mordecai's day. Esther tells how Mordecai wrote to the Jews to keep the fourteenth and fifteenth of Adar as days of joy. Second Maccabees sets a new feast beside it and calls the older one by Mordecai's name.

- 2Macc.15.36 carries: Mordecai, Adar

## `esth-o02` Robed, mounted and proclaimed

- **Gen.41.42-Gen.41.43 → Esth.6.11** · parallel · strength 2 · draft · fingerprint `529c97b3a8df599e`
- From: Joseph in Egypt (`gen-17`)
- To: The Sleepless King (`esth-07`)
- Order: older end unknown; no use claimed; basis unstated

> Genesis 41:42–43 and Esther 6:11 both tell of a Hebrew in a foreign court honoured by the king with royal clothing, a ride through the city and a herald crying out before him. Pharaoh dresses Joseph in fine linen, puts his ring on his hand and has him ride in the second chariot; the king has Haman robe Mordecai, mount him on the royal horse and proclaim his honour, and later gives Mordecai his ring. The words for clothing and riding are the same (below).
