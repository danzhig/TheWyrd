## `col-l02` The same names in two letters

- From: The New Life; the Household; Greetings (`col-02`, library)
- To: Paul’s Appeal for Onesimus (`phlm-01`, library)

> Colossians 4:10–14 and Philemon 1:23–24 both tell of the same companions sending greetings with Paul: Epaphras, Mark, Aristarchus, Demas and Luke. Colossians adds that Epaphras wrestles in prayer for them and that Luke is the beloved physician. The shared names tie the two letters to one time and place.
