## `ruth-o03` The sandal at the gate

- **Deut.25.7-Deut.25.10 → Ruth.4.7-Ruth.4.8** · echo · strength 2 · verified (approved 2026-11-20) · fingerprint `d30d1ad15a40dd21`
- From: Justice and First Fruits (`deut-x316`)

> Deuteronomy 25:7–10 and Ruth 4:7–8 both tell of a sandal taken off to settle a kinsman's duty before the elders at the town gate. Deuteronomy sets the shame of the sandal on a brother who refuses to raise up a name for his dead brother. In Ruth the nearer kinsman gives up his right to redeem by drawing off his own sandal, a custom of former times, with the same word for sandal (below).
