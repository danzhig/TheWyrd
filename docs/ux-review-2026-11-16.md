# Usability review, 2026-11-16

A walk through every page and mode on a desktop (1440×900) and a phone (iPhone 13), after Phases 4–6 added Themes, Threads and the library: the Vault (Spiral, Themes, Threads, List), a book map, the reader (settings, threads, arriving from a theme or a thread), a bridge, the path, About, Sources and Search. No page logged an error. What was found, what was changed, and what is left for the owner to decide.

## For the owner to decide

The owner chose 1, 3, 4 and 5 (2026-11-16), done in the follow-up: one Continue control that opens the reading; themes and threads in search; "← Home"; the view switch in the page in List mode on phones (and usable there). Item 2 is left as it is.

1. **Continue and Resume.** The Vault has both: Continue flies the camera to your current book; "Resume · …" opens the reading itself. Two controls for one idea. Suggestion: one button, "Continue reading", that opens the reading, with the camera flight dropped or moved to the book's medallion.
2. **Threads are dense.** With 170 connections the web of 93 arcs is busy, especially on phones. Suggestions: tapping a book in Threads shows only its arcs (others fade); or the filter starts on quotations and named references only.
3. **Search** covers the text of every section, but not the themes or the connections. It could list a theme or a thread when a search names one.
4. **"All books"** is the back link on About, Sources, the path and Search; it leads to the Vault. "The Vault" or "Home" may be clearer.
5. **Phones, List mode.** The Spiral · Themes · Threads switch stays fixed at the foot of the screen over the list. It could move into the page above the list while List is on.
